Hello!
I’m MR.BONE

In a post-apocalyptic world, where humanity's downfall came at the hands of a mysterious zombie plague, MR. BONE emerged as an unlikely hero. Once a brilliant scientist, his untimely death and accidental resurrection left him caught between life and death. But unlike the mindless zombies, MR. BONE retained his intelligence, wit, and a strong sense of humor.

In the desolate wastelands where humanity once thrived, a new breed of zombie has emerged—relentless hunters known as The Necrochasers. Unlike mindless hordes that aimlessly roam the ruins, these undead predators have purpose. Their hunger is not just for flesh; it’s for the skull of MR. BONE, a target borne out of his uncanny resistance and brilliance. The Necrochasers are remnants of a failed experimental project led by scientists who sought to conquer the zombie plague with cybernetic enhancements. Instead, their experiments birthed creatures with razor-sharp instincts, insatiable curiosity, and a singular mission: capture MR. BONE, the anomaly who survived death, retained intelligence, and, most dangerously, understands the origins of the plague itself. These zombie hunters possess heightened speed, tactical intelligence, and even rudimentary communication skills. Each one wears remnants of their human past—lab coats fused with decaying flesh, cybernetic limbs, and makeshift armor. Their grotesque appearance is a haunting reminder of humanity’s downfall, combining the chilling familiarity of human features with the deforming decay of undeath. But MR. BONE, with his quick wit and mechanical ingenuity, remains an elusive prey. He uses his environment—the twisted cityscapes, hidden alleyways, and broken skyscrapers—to set traps and escape paths. Every encounter with The Necrochasers is a deadly game of strategy, where MR. BONE’s survival depends on outsmarting these relentless hunters rather than overpowering them.
