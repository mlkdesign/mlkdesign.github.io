Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data this portfolio receives while you browse it, why, who helps process it and what rights you have. It describes how this edition of the website actually works today.

Effective from 31 October 2026 · Last updated 3 November 2026

01

Introduction

This is the Upwork edition of the portfolio of Jack Milka (Yevhenii Milka), a UI/UX and digital product designer, at https://mlkdesign.github.io/upwork/. It shows case studies and a CV for clients who found Jack on Upwork.

The website has no contact form and no accounts. You cannot buy services, pay or sign contracts on it; all communication and work happen on Upwork.

03

Information We Collect

Technical data

When you open the website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and the services that deliver the site’s JavaScript libraries (unpkg.com, esm.sh) receive your IP address and browser information, as any web server does. Opening the CV works the same way.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

Only after you click “accept” in the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 records which pages you view, the device, browser and approximate location it derives from your connection, and clicks on a fixed set of elements: opening a case study, opening a project’s live site and opening the CV.

Visit statistics (Microsoft Clarity)

Microsoft Clarity records how visitors use the pages: clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, which it turns into heatmaps and session recordings, together with the pages viewed, the device, the browser and the approximate location it derives from your connection. Clarity does not wait for the cookie banner.

We do not use advertising pixels or build advertising profiles.

Messages on Upwork

What you send through Upwork is processed by Upwork under its own privacy policy. This website does not receive it.

04

How We Use Information

  • To understand which work and pages interest visitors (Google Analytics, only with your consent).
  • To see how visitors use the site and fix what gets in their way (Microsoft Clarity).
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not use this data for marketing, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

09

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we process your data and give you a copy (access);
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data where there is no reason to keep it;
  • restrict processing while a question about it is being resolved;
  • stop processing based on our legitimate interest (objection);
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Send your request through Upwork messages. We reply within one month and may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you. You can withdraw your analytics consent yourself at any time with the “Cookie Settings” link at the bottom of every page.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in the EU/EEA, to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work; in Ukraine, to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. You can also go to court.
