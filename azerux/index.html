Frequently Asked Questions

What is a DDoS attack?

A DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attack floods a website, server or network with junk traffic from many sources at once, so that legitimate users can no longer reach it.

Get consult

What are the risks of DDoS attacks?

DDoS-Guard mitigates the impact of malicious bots and spam of feedback forms, as well as reports vulnerabilities and threats from the OWASP Top-10 list. In our blog, you can find a list of the most popular threats that DDoS-Guard helps protect from. In case of an attack of unknown type or large in volume, our engineers will ensure timely adaptation of the scrubbing system to minimize the malicious impact.

Get consult

What cyber threats do DDoS-Guard services protect from?

Volumetric and protocol attacks at L3–L4, application-layer attacks at L7, malicious bots, spam of feedback forms and threats from the OWASP Top-10 list.

Get consult

How does the DDoS protection system work?

We use Reverse Proxy technology with a DNS A record change: all traffic passes through our scrubbing network, malicious requests are filtered out, and only legitimate traffic reaches your protected IP and web server.

Get consult

How does DDoS-Guard provide high-quality services?

A proprietary scrubbing network, interconnected Tier III data centers around the world, 24/7 monitoring by our engineers and detailed statistics on mitigated attacks in the Client Area.

Get consult
