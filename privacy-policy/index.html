03

Information We Collect

When you send a project request

  • Name — as you enter it.
  • Email address — so we can reply.
  • Project details — the content of your message and anything you choose to include in it.

The form has no phone number field and no file upload. Please do not include sensitive information (for example health data or identity documents) in your message.

Technical data

  • When you submit the form, our form service (Formspree) receives the technical data that comes with any web request, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and uses it to deliver the message and filter spam.
  • When you open the website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and the services that deliver the site's JavaScript libraries (unpkg.com, esm.sh) receive your IP address and browser information, as any web server does.

When you contact us elsewhere

If you write to us by email, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance or Upwork, we receive what you send us there. Those platforms process your data under their own privacy policies.

Visit statistics on the personal portfolio

Only on /milka-cv/ and /upwork/, and only after you click “accept” in the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 records which pages you view, the device, browser and approximate location it derives from your connection, and clicks on a fixed set of elements (for example opening the request form or a case study). It never receives what you type into the form.

Visit statistics on the studio pages

On the studio’s own pages (the home page, case studies and these legal pages) Microsoft Clarity records how visitors use them: clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, which it turns into heatmaps and session recordings, together with the pages viewed, the device, the browser and the approximate location it derives from your connection. The project request form is marked so that Clarity does not record what you type into it. The project demos and the personal portfolio do not load Clarity.

Nowhere do we use advertising pixels or build advertising profiles.

04

How We Use Information

  • To read and answer your request.
  • To discuss your project and prepare a proposal or estimate.
  • To agree, carry out and invoice a project if we start working together.
  • To keep the website and the form secure and working, including spam protection.
  • To keep the records Ukrainian law requires once a contract exists.

We do not use your request for newsletters or other marketing, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

On the personal portfolio, and only with your consent, we use visit statistics to understand which work and pages interest visitors. On the studio pages we use Clarity’s heatmaps and session recordings to see how visitors use the site and to fix what gets in their way.

07

Data Retention

  • Requests that do not lead to a project — we delete them from the mailbox and from Formspree 12 months after our last exchange.
  • Requests that lead to a project — we keep the correspondence for as long as the contract runs and afterwards for as long as Ukrainian tax and accounting law requires, or as needed to settle a legal claim.
  • Hosting and delivery logs — kept by the providers listed above under their own retention rules.
  • Visit statistics — Google Analytics keeps event data for the retention period set in our account (2 months, the GA4 default), then deletes it.
  • Studio page statistics — Microsoft Clarity keeps recordings and statistics for the retention periods Microsoft sets for the service, then deletes them.

The 12-month period is our own rule, not a legal requirement. You can ask us to delete your request earlier at any time.

We are based in Ukraine. When you send us a request from the EU, the EEA or anywhere else, you send it directly to us in Ukraine.

These providers state that they protect international transfers with the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Ukraine does not currently have an EU adequacy decision.

The website is served only over HTTPS, and the form sends your request to Formspree over an encrypted connection. Requests are read in a password-protected mailbox that only the people who answer them can access. The website does not store your request in the page, in its address or in your browser.

No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
