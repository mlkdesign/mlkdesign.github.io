Relocation

Changing your place of residence is a very important decision that requires serious preparation. You need to weigh all the pros and cons of the chosen country, because the procedure for obtaining a residence permit, permanent residence and citizenship differs everywhere. Countries around the world offer different grounds for obtaining permission to live there, one of which may suit you perfectly.


The right grounds and a sound strategy will save you time and nerves, making the move more pleasant and minimising the chance of refusal. Citizenship by investment, a residence permit based on a business in the country, permanent residence after a certain period of continuous residence, family reunification or something else?
