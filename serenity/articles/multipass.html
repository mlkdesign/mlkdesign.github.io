What is MultiPass?

You can open an account abroad with the MultiPass payment system. MultiPass is a British payment system formerly known as DynaPay.

The MultiPass head office is in London (United Kingdom). The payment system has been operating since 2017 and successfully opens accounts for companies all over the world. MultiPass is convenient online banking that is part of the international holding DYNINNO Group, founded in 2004.

Why open an account with MultiPass?

MultiPass offers a number of advantages that may attract business owners. This payment system offers a single account for all of a business’s international payment needs.

Let’s look at some of the advantages in more detail:

1.Possibility to open a multi-currency account

MultiPass offers accounts in more than 30 world currencies.

2.Possibility for non-residents to open an account

Non-residents can open an account with MultiPass just like residents; they only need to provide additional documents.

3.Remote procedure

The whole account opening procedure at MultiPass is remote: you do not need to travel to the United Kingdom.

4.Fast account opening

A MultiPass account will be ready in 1–2 weeks depending on the case, and sometimes opening an account takes just 24 hours.

5.Convenient online account management

A MultiPass account is very easy to manage online through your personal dashboard.

6.Unique IBAN

The account holder at MultiPass receives a unique IBAN for transfers to the USA, the EU and the United Kingdom.

7.Communication with a personal manager

Any questions related to both opening and managing a MultiPass account can be resolved directly with a personal manager

How to open an account with MultiPass?

To open an account with MultiPass, you need to go to their website and provide the necessary information and documents.

The exact list of required documents will depend on the details of your application. As a rule, after you provide basic details about your company, MultiPass staff will request a specific list of documents. There is no single list, because every company has its own specifics, such as its type of activity, company structure, residency of the founders and the geography of payments (where payments will come from and go to).
