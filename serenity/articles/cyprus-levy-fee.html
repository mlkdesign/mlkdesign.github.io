What is the levy fee?

The levy fee in Cyprus is an annual state corporate fee that must be paid on time by all companies registered in Cyprus, whether or not they have been active.

Interesting:

Company registration in Cyprus originally became popular because of the favourable business climate and attractive conditions for doing business. Cyprus has a strategically advantageous location, a loyal tax system with preferential conditions and many other advantages.

The levy fee in Cyprus is 350 EUR. The fee was introduced in 2011 with the adoption of a new law, under which all registered resident companies in Cyprus must pay this amount to the state budget.

When must a company pay the levy fee in Cyprus?

Companies in Cyprus must pay the levy fee no later than 30 June each year.

However, the following factors should be taken into account:

  • If a company is registered after 30 June, the newly created company does not have to pay the annual fee for its first year;
  • Companies in liquidation that started before the end of June do not have to pay the levy fee for the current year;
  • Under the Companies Law of the Republic of Cyprus, organisations registered in the jurisdiction pay the fee to the Department of the Registrar.

Penalties for not paying the levy fee in Cyprus

For non-payment or late payment of the levy fee in Cyprus, the following penalties are imposed on the company:

  • 10% if the delay is up to 2 calendar months (until 31.08 — 385 EUR);
  • 30% if the payment is delayed by 2 to 5 months (until 30.11 — 490 EUR in total).

Important:

Non-payment of the annual fee may also lead to the company being struck off the Cyprus Register of Companies.

Thus, a company in Cyprus must fulfil its obligation to pay the levy fee. This fee is the most important contribution for the full functioning of the company and for keeping it active.

Do you need to be present in person to pay the levy fee?

You do not have to visit the jurisdiction in person to pay the levy fee in Cyprus. The fee can be paid in one of the following ways:

  • By debit/credit card online via JCC (for 2018 and later);
  • On the official website of the Registrar of Companies — via DRCOR electronic filing.

Interesting:

The website also lets you check the current status of all paid/overdue annual fee amounts for any Cypriot company.

At the cash desk of the Registrar of Companies in cash or by cheque.

Conclusion

Thus, the levy fee in Cyprus of 350 EUR is paid by all companies registered in Cyprus, whether or not they carry out any activity.

Failure to pay the levy fee exposes the company to penalties, the inability to obtain important documents (for example, a Certificate of Good Standing) and even removal from the Register.
