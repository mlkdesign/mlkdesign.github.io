Our specialists will help you acquire your own company with an open account

Already have your own business and need to expand its scope? To avoid wasting time and effort on registering a legal entity, you can buy a ready-made company.

The companies are already registered; you only need to register the ownership to the new owner and change the director. The client will receive all the papers needed for the legal entity to operate.


Ready-made companies are in demand among entrepreneurs who need to take part in a tender but whose own company does not meet the participant requirements. Since the company already has an account, you will not have to spend time opening one.
