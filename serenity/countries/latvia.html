Commercial offer for company registrationin Latvia

General information

Latvia is one of the largest financial and cultural centres of the modern world. Having your own business in this jurisdiction is prestigious and respectable. As a rule, such a company does not raise doubts among partners.

Many people mistakenly believe that Latvia is an offshore zone — this is not the case. Latvia is a low-tax jurisdiction. The specifics of local legislation make it possible to significantly optimise financial flows outside the jurisdiction.

Types of legal forms available for registration in Latvia

LTD (Limited Company)

Requirements:
An office in Latvia is required (we provide a correspondence address service in London). In business practice the most common share capital for a company in Latvia is 1,000 pounds sterling (paying up the share capital is optional).
Shareholders:
The minimum number of shareholders is one(individuals or legal entities).
Directors:
The minimum number of directors is one (an individual).Citizenship and residency do not matter.The director and the shareholder can be the same person.
Important:
We offer nominee shareholder and director services for doing business.Latvia has an open register of shareholders and directors. The information is publicly available.

LP (Limited Partnership)

Requirements:
An office in Latvia is required (we provide a correspondence address service in London).
Report:
Partnerships in Latvia do not file an annual report.
Important:
An LP in Latvia is not a separate legal entity, meaning that tax is paid not by the partnership but by each partner separately in their own jurisdiction. The partnership’s profit can be distributed proportionally between the partners, and they must declare it in their own country.

* Please note that Latvia has concluded a large number of double taxation treaties with other countries.

Taxation

LTD

It is a resident from the moment the company is registered.

It pays corporate tax on an extraterritorial basis. That is, if your company is registered in Latvia, it pays profit tax on its activities both in Latvia and abroad.

The corporate tax rate in Latvia is 19% for 2021/22.

The VAT rate in Latvia is 20%.

LP

The limited partnership itself is not subject to taxation in Latvia.

The LP’s profit is distributed between its partners, who must pay taxes in the country of their tax residence.

If the partnership has foreign participants and does not operate in Latvia, it has no tax obligations there, and the participants must fulfil their tax obligations themselves in the country of their residence.

Registration procedure

1. Before registering a company, you need to determine:


  1. The company name to check its uniqueness (at least three names). It must end with an indication of the legal form (“Limited” or “Ltd”).
  2. The company structure (director, shareholder, beneficiary, attorney).
  3. The amount of share capital and the distribution of shares between shareholders.
  4. The company’s type of activity (SIC).

2. Documents must be provided for directors/shareholders/beneficiaries/attorneys:


A copy of the passport with the registration page, as well as documents confirming the address (UB).

3. Submitting the company registration application


(registering a new company takes about three working days).

The set of documents handed over to the client after registering a company in Latvia includes:

Certificate of Incorporation

Articles of Association

Memorandum of Association

Minutes of the first Meeting

Resolution of shareholders

Share Certificate

Apostille of documents (on request)

Company seal (on request)

Power of attorney (on request)

Reporting

Every company in Latvia must file an annual return with Companies House every year.

If the annual return is not filed, the Registrar may assume that the company in Latvia is no longer doing business and strike it off the register, as well as impose fines and penalties.

Annual maintenance

  • 2. Administrative services of a professional agent (annual compliance)
  • 3. Nominee service costs
  • 4. Help with preparing the annual return (no audit required)
  • 5. Annual company renewal
